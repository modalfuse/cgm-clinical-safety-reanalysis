# Privacy and License

## Privacy boundary

The distributed result files are table-level aggregates selected to reproduce manuscript display values. They contain model, cohort, threshold-policy, count, rate, interval, and discrimination summaries. They contain no participant identifiers, observation times, row-level labels, row-level forecasts, or longitudinal indices.

The public datasets are obtained from their original providers. Their source records and individual prediction records are governed by provider access, use, attribution, and redistribution conditions and are not redistributed here.
