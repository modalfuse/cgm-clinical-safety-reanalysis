# Data Sources

The manuscript analyzes five public cohorts curated by GlucoBench (`iglu`, `dubosson`, `hall`, `colas`, and `weinstock`) and two public Shanghai cohorts (ShanghaiT1DM and ShanghaiT2DM).

- GlucoBench: Sergazinov R et al. *International Conference on Learning Representations*. 2024. https://openreview.net/forum?id=cUSNs8nGaV
- D1NAMO (`dubosson`): Dubosson F et al. *Inform Med Unlocked*. 2018;13:92–100. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.imu.2018.09.003
- `weinstock`: Weinstock RS et al. *Diabetes Care*. 2016;39:603–610. https://doi.org/10.2337/dc15-1426
- Shanghai cohorts: Zhao Q et al. *Sci Data*. 2023;10:35. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-01940-7

The aggregate files in `derived/` contain manuscript display values organized by cohort, model, analysis role, and operating policy. Dataset access and use remain subject to each original provider’s terms.
